Samuel Agada Peterson Otor — FUTA Biomedical Engineering

Biomedical Breakdown started as a personal learning project. As a biomedical engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), I realized many biomedical concepts were either overcomplicated or poorly explained online. I wanted a space where difficult ideas — from ECG systems to neural interfaces — could be explored clearly, honestly, and with curiosity.

This platform documents my growth as a student, researcher, and builder. Rather than pretending to know everything, Biomedical Breakdown is about learning publicly, asking better questions, and making biomedical engineering more approachable for students like me.

My interests currently revolve around AI in healthcare, biomedical sensors, wearable medical systems, neural interfaces, and the future of medical technology across Africa. I'm especially fascinated by how engineering can improve diagnostics, accessibility, and long-term patient care.

Beyond academics, I also care deeply about communication — simplifying difficult ideas without stripping away the real science behind them. That balance between technical depth and clarity is what this project is built on.

Biomedical Breakdown is gradually evolving into more than a website. The long-term vision is to build an educational and research-driven platform where biomedical engineering, AI, health technology, and scientific communication become more accessible to students across Africa and beyond.
